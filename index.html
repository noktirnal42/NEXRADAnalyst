NEXRAD Analyst Pro

See the storm. Understand the data.

A native Mac application for analyzing NEXRAD Level II weather radar data with multi-pane display, Metal-accelerated 3D volume rendering, storm-relative velocity, real-time NWS warnings, and on-device AI weather analysis.

Explore Features Get Support

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Designed for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Built for analysis.

NEXRAD Analyst Pro brings professional-grade radar tools to the Mac, powered by Apple technologies and designed for meteorologists, storm chasers, and weather enthusiasts.

📡

Level II Data

Direct access to NEXRAD Level II base data from 160+ WSR-88D radar sites across the US, streamed from NOAA and AWS archives.

🖥

Multi-Pane Display

Analyze multiple products simultaneously with synced 1-up, 2-up, and 4-up pane layouts on a single map view.

🌀

3D Volume Rendering

Metal-accelerated GPU ray-casting transforms volumetric radar data into interactive 3D storm visualizations at 60 fps.

💨

Storm-Relative Velocity

Configurable storm motion vectors for SRM analysis, revealing rotation signatures hidden in base velocity data.

⚠

Live NWS Warnings

Real-time warning polygons from the National Weather Service, color-coded by severity with local severe-weather notifications.

🤖

AI Weather Analysis

Optional on-device weather situation analysis using Core ML and Apple Foundation Models where available, with rule-based fallback on supported macOS releases.

📊

Inspector Panel

Hodograph wind profiles, atmospheric soundings from radiosonde data, and detailed volume scan information in a dedicated sidebar.

🗺

METAR Observations

Surface weather station plots with standard meteorological station model display including wind barbs, sky cover, and conditions.

🔬

Derived Products

Echo tops, VIL, MEHS, POSH, and spectrum width analysis computed from full-volume radar data.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Full keyboard-driven workflow with shortcuts for site selection, products, overlays, layouts, playback, and more.

Siri Shortcuts

Load your favorite radar sites and switch products by voice using Siri on macOS.

Drag & Drop

Import NEXRAD Level II archive files (.ar2v) by dragging them onto the window. Export radar snapshots as PNG.

Spotlight Search

Find any of 160+ radar sites directly from macOS Spotlight without opening the app.

SharePlay

Collaborate on storm analysis in real time with shared radar views and synchronized cursors via FaceTime.

WeatherKit Integration

Current surface conditions, hourly forecasts, and severe weather alerts overlaid directly on the radar display.
