Built for analysis.

NEXRAD Analyst brings professional-grade radar analysis tools to the Mac, powered by Apple technologies and designed for meteorologists, storm chasers, and weather enthusiasts.

📡

Level II Data

Direct access to NEXRAD Level II base data from 160+ radar sites across the US, streamed from NOAA and AWS archives.

🖥️

Multi-Pane Display

Analyze multiple products simultaneously with synced 1-up, 2-up, and 4-up pane layouts on a single map view.

🌀

3D Volume Rendering

Metal-accelerated GPU ray-casting transforms volumetric radar data into interactive 3D storm visualizations at 60fps.

💨

Storm-Relative Velocity

Configurable storm motion vectors for SRM analysis, revealing rotation signatures hidden in base velocity data.

⚠️

Live NWS Warnings

Real-time warning polygons from the National Weather Service color-coded by severity, with critical tornado alert notifications.

🤖

AI Weather Analysis

On-device weather situation analysis using Apple Foundation Models, with threat assessment and safety recommendations.

📊

Inspector Panel

Hodograph wind profiles, atmospheric soundings, and detailed volume scan information in a dedicated analysis sidebar.

🗺️

METAR Observations

Surface weather station plots with standard meteorological station model display including wind barbs, sky cover, and conditions.

🔬

Derived Products

Echo tops, VIL, MEHS, POSH, and spectrum width analysis computed from full-volume radar data.
