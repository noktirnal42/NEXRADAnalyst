Getting Started

1

Select a Radar Site

Click the site selector in the toolbar to browse 160+ NEXRAD radar sites. Search by site ID (e.g., KTLX), city name, or state. Star your favorites for quick access.

2

Choose a Product

Use the product picker to switch between reflectivity, velocity, storm-relative velocity, spectrum width, and dual-polarization products (ZDR, ρHV, KDP, ΦDP).

3

Analyze the Data

Use multi-pane layouts, the inspector panel, playback controls, and warning overlays to analyze weather patterns. Hover over the radar display for precise readout values.

Features Guide

Multi-Pane Display

View up to four radar products simultaneously with synced map navigation. Use the layout picker in the toolbar to switch between 1-up, 2-up, and 4-up views. Each pane can display a different product while maintaining synchronized pan and zoom. Requires Pro subscription.

3D Volume Rendering

Toggle the 3D view from the toolbar to see a Metal-accelerated volumetric rendering of the entire radar volume. Click and drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. The performance overlay shows FPS and render time. Requires Pro subscription.

Storm-Relative Velocity (SRM)

Enable SRM from the toolbar to subtract storm motion from velocity data, making rotation signatures easier to identify. Click the storm motion editor to set direction and speed based on storm movement. Requires Pro subscription.

Inspector Panel

Open the inspector from the toolbar to view hodograph wind profiles, atmospheric soundings (fetched from radiosonde data), and detailed volume scan information including VCP, elevation angles, and coordinates. Requires Pro subscription.

NWS Warning Overlays

Toggle warning polygons from the toolbar to see active National Weather Service warnings. Tornado warnings appear in red, severe thunderstorm in orange, flash flood in green, and special weather statements in yellow.

METAR Surface Observations

Enable METAR display from the toolbar to see nearby surface weather stations plotted using standard meteorological station models with wind barbs, sky cover symbols, temperature, dewpoint, and pressure.

Playback & Timeline

Use the timeline scrubber at the bottom of the display to step through recent volume scans. Use the play button for animated playback, or step forward/backward through individual frames.

AI Weather Analysis

Use the AI analysis button in the toolbar to generate an on-device weather situation summary. The analysis includes current conditions assessment, threat level, and safety recommendations. Requires ML Analysis Pack.

Siri Shortcuts

NEXRAD Analyst supports Siri Shortcuts for loading radar sites and switching products. Add shortcuts through the Shortcuts app on macOS to quickly access your most-used radar sites by voice.

Drag & Drop

Drag NEXRAD Level II archive files (.ar2v) directly onto the application window to load them. You can also drag radar snapshots out of the app to share them.

Troubleshooting

Radar data is not loading

NEXRAD Analyst requires an internet connection to fetch radar data from NOAA and AWS. Check your network connection and try clicking the refresh button. If a specific site is unavailable, the radar may be undergoing maintenance.

3D rendering performance is slow

3D volume rendering uses Metal GPU acceleration. Performance depends on your GPU. Close other GPU-intensive applications and try reducing the window size. Apple silicon Macs provide the best rendering performance.

METAR data is missing for some stations

METAR observations are fetched from the NWS API and depend on station reporting. Not all stations near a radar site may have current observations available. Observations update approximately every hour.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open NEXRAD Analyst, go to the Pro subscription view (Pro badge in toolbar), and tap "Restore Purchases." Your subscriptions and purchases sync automatically through your Apple ID.
